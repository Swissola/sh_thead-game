<objective>
Close the blocker gap from `02-UAT.md` test 8: `useRoomSubscription`'s `postgres_changes` channel
has no subscribe-status callback and no `CHANNEL_ERROR`/`TIMED_OUT`/`CLOSED` handling anywhere -
confirmed via a codebase-wide grep. If the underlying WebSocket silently drops (tab backgrounding,
a network blip, an idle period), nothing detects it or resubscribes; the client's screen simply
stops reflecting new game-state broadcasts forever, with no user-visible signal, until a full
manual rejoin forces a resync. Confirmed live: a PC's screen stopped updating after an idle
period during a real two-device test, while the server's own `moves` audit table (room ZABYDB,
versions 58-72) proved every move had actually landed correctly - the desync was purely
client-side and purely about the Realtime subscription going stale.

`usePresence.ts`'s `channel.subscribe((status) => { if (status === 'SUBSCRIBED') {...} })` is the
only existing precedent in this codebase for reacting to a channel's subscribe status; this plan
extends that same pattern to the room-data channel, plus the recovery behaviour presence does not
need (presence is ephemeral and self-resyncs for free on its own next `sync` event - the room-data
channel carries the one-shot `UPDATE`/`INSERT` deliveries that D-11's reconciliation depends on
being complete, so a gap in its coverage cannot be shrugged off the same way).

Purpose: MPLAY-02 replaced the old localStorage poll specifically to remove a single point of
staleness (RESEARCH.md Pattern 1); a Realtime subscription that can silently die and never recover
reintroduces exactly that staleness, just with a longer and less predictable failure window and no
polling fallback to eventually correct it. A permanently frozen game-state view is a harder failure
than the poll it replaced, because nothing about the UI signals it has happened.

Output: a subscribe-status callback on the room-data channel's `.subscribe(...)` call that detects
a dropped connection, resubscribes with a capped exponential backoff (that only resets after a
genuine dwell period of connectivity, not on every brief reconnect) instead of a tight loop, and -
specifically on recovery from a genuine drop, not on every routine subscribe - performs a one-off
direct read of the room row. That read's reconciliation check is gated on whether this client
genuinely had a move outstanding at the moment the connection dropped (a snapshot that survives
02-16's separate 8-second pending-move safety net clearing the live flag mid-outage), not on the
live `hasPendingMove()` value at refetch time - so a client that lost a real race still gets the
toast even after the safety net has fired, while an idle bystander whose channel merely happened to
drop while an opponent moved never does, exactly preserving 02-16's own guarantee for this new
delivery path.
</objective>
